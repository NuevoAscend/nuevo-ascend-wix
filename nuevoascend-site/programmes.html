1:1 Leadership Coaching

Personal, honest coaching built on two decades of real leadership — not textbooks.

This is coaching for people who are serious about progression. Whether you are stepping into management for the first time, pushing for a senior promotion, or rebuilding confidence after a setback — this programme gives you a dedicated, experienced thinking partner who has genuinely been where you are trying to go.

👤 Format 1:1 Sessions
via Zoom or In-Person
📅 Duration Single Session to 8-Week Programme
⏱ Session Length 60–90 Minutes per Session
🎯 Start Rolling Intake — Start Anytime

Who This Is For

  • First-time managers finding their feet in a leadership role
  • Professionals preparing for interviews or promotion boards
  • Mid-career leaders wanting to step up to senior management
  • Individuals from underrepresented backgrounds navigating corporate environments
  • Anyone who has been overlooked and wants to understand why — and what to do about it
  • Leaders rebuilding confidence after redundancy, a difficult role, or a career setback
  • Retail and customer experience professionals seeking to transition into leadership

What's Included

  • Dedicated 1:1 sessions with an experienced senior leader
  • Personalised leadership development plan created in session one

  • Interview and promotion preparation — mock sessions and feedback
  • Post-session action notes sent within 24 hours
  • WhatsApp support between sessions (on programme packages)
  • End-of-programme review and next-steps roadmap

Young Leaders Programme

For the next generation of managers who deserve the investment that built this business.

This programme exists because of a simple truth: the founder was a manager at sixteen with no mentor, no roadmap, and no one who looked like him at the top. He figured it out — and never forgot what it cost to do so without support. The Young Leaders Programme is the investment he never had, now available to every young person who deserves it.

🎓 Age Range 16–25 Years Old
📍 Delivery In-Person, Virtual or Hybrid
⏱ Duration 4–12 Week Programmes
💷 Funding Grant Options Available

Who This Is For

  • Young people aged 16–25 in their first management or team leader role
  • Sixth form and college students exploring leadership as a career path
  • Apprentices and graduates entering corporate environments for the first time
  • Young professionals from underrepresented backgrounds in early-career roles
  • Youth organisations, schools, and charities supporting young talent
  • Companies with graduate or apprenticeship schemes seeking structured development

What's Included

  • Group coaching sessions with the Nuevo Ascend founder
  • Personal leadership profile — strengths, blind spots, and development areas
  • Career mapping sessions — building a realistic plan for progression
  • Practical modules on confidence, communication, and professional presence
  • Mentoring from senior leaders in the Nuevo Ascend network
  • CV, interview, and personal branding support
  • Certificate of completion and ongoing community access

Programme Themes

  • What leadership really means — and why it starts with you
  • Building confidence in environments that were not built for you
  • How to earn respect as a young manager
  • Understanding workplace culture and how to navigate it
  • Setting goals and holding yourself accountable
  • Presenting yourself with purpose — in interviews, at work, and online

Delivery Partners

  • Secondary schools and sixth forms
  • Further education colleges
  • Universities and student unions
  • Apprenticeship programme providers
  • Youth charities and social enterprises
  • Corporate ERG (Employee Resource Group) programmes
  • Prince's Trust partner organisations

"I was a 16-year-old Black boy leading a team of adults. Nobody gave me a manual. This programme is the manual I wish I had — now given to every young person who deserves it."

Speaking Engagements

A story that does not sound like anyone else on your stage — because it isn't.

From paper boy at eleven to shaking hands with Apple's Tim Cook. From manager at sixteen to Black British Business Award winner at the FT. The Nuevo Ascend founder brings a speaking style that is honest, powerful, and impossible to forget — built on a life story that proves what is possible when you refuse to accept the ceiling others set for you.

🎤 Format Keynote, Panel or Workshop
⏱ Duration 20 mins to Full Day
📍 Location UK-Wide & International
👥 Audience 20 to 2,000+ Attendees

Signature Talks

  • "The 16-Year-Old Manager" — how a young Black man led a team before most had their first job, and what it taught him about real leadership
  • "From Paper Boy to Tim Cook" — the full journey from age 11 to Apple's global stage, the BBBA, and building two businesses
  • "Leadership Beyond the Title" — why operational credibility and consistency matter more than any job title
  • "Representation in the Room" — building diverse leadership pipelines that work authentically, not performatively
  • "Building a Business While Working Full Time" — the real story behind founding Nuevo Noir while holding a senior corporate career

Who Books Nuevo Ascend

  • Corporate ERG and diversity & inclusion events
  • Leadership conferences and business summits
  • Universities and student leadership societies
  • Graduate and apprenticeship induction programmes
  • Schools and colleges inspiring young people about careers
  • Black Business Network events and BBBA affiliated gatherings
  • TEDx and thought leadership platforms
  • Retail sector events and forums

What's Included

  • Pre-event discovery call to tailor talk to your audience and theme
  • Fully prepared, professionally delivered keynote or panel contribution
  • Audience Q&A where requested
  • Speaker biography and professional headshot for event materials
  • Optional post-talk workshop add-on available
  • Follow-up resources for attendees

Why This Talk Lands

  • Not motivational fluff — a real story with real stakes and real lessons
  • Speaks directly to audiences from underrepresented backgrounds without being tokenistic
  • Equally powerful for corporate leadership audiences, students, and young professionals
  • Award-winning credibility — BBBA 2024, FT-sponsored — gives weight to every word
  • Memorable, human, and impossible to forget
