Founder's Story

Built From Real
Leadership

Nuevo Ascend was not built in a business school. It was built on two decades of real operational leadership — multi-site retail at Sainsbury's and Argos, senior customer experience at Apple, and diversity programme delivery across the UK and Ireland.

The founder became a store manager at 16 years old. He went on to stand on a stage with Apple CEO Tim Cook. In 2024, he won the Black British Business Award, sponsored by the Financial Times. Now he's packaging every lesson from that journey into coaching, consultancy, and programmes that help others do the same.
