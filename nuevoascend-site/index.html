Founder's Story

Built From Real
Leadership

Nuevo Ascend was not built in a business school. It was built on two decades of real operational leadership — multi-site retail at Sainsbury's and Argos, including serving as Strategic Advisor to CEO Simon Roberts on inclusion matters, and Senior Manager at Apple across Operations, CX and B2B — head of one of the largest diversity networks across the UK.

The founder became a manager at 16 years old. He went on to host visits with Apple CEO Tim Cook and interview Global Editorial Head of Hip-Hop and R&B, Ebro Darden. In 2024, he won the Black British Business Award, sponsored by the Financial Times.
