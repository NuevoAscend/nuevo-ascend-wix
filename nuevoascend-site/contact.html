Common Questions

Questions, Answered

How long does the discovery call take?

A free 20-minute conversation. No sales pitch and no commitment required — just an honest conversation about your goals.

Do I need a coaching qualification?

Not at all. Our programmes are open to anyone at any stage of their career — from first-time managers to senior leaders.

How are sessions delivered?

1:1 coaching sessions are delivered via Zoom or in person in London. Workshops can be on-site, remote, or hybrid.

Can my organisation pay by invoice?

Yes. Corporate clients, schools, colleges and charities can all be invoiced directly via Nuevo Ascend Ltd.

Is the Young Leaders Programme for schools?

Absolutely. It's designed for schools, colleges, apprenticeship programmes and youth charities. Grant funding options available.

How quickly can a workshop be booked?

Workshops can typically be booked and delivered within 2–4 weeks, subject to availability and a pre-workshop discovery call.
