Common Questions

Questions, Answered

How long does the discovery call take?
The discovery call is a free 20-minute conversation. There is no sales pitch and no commitment required — it is simply an honest conversation about your goals and how Nuevo Ascend can help.
Do I need a coaching qualification to work with you?
Not at all. Our coaching and programmes are open to anyone at any stage of their career — from first-time managers to senior leaders. The only requirement is a genuine commitment to your own development.
How are sessions delivered — in person or online?
1:1 coaching sessions are delivered via Zoom or in person in London, depending on your preference. Corporate workshops and consultancy can be delivered on-site at your organisation, remotely, or in a hybrid format.
Can my organisation pay by invoice?
Yes. Corporate clients, schools, colleges, and charities can all be invoiced directly. We issue formal invoices via Nuevo Ascend Ltd and can provide all documentation required for procurement processes.
Is the Young Leaders Programme available for schools?
Absolutely. The Young Leaders Programme is specifically designed to be delivered through schools, colleges, apprenticeship programmes, and youth charities. Grant funding and subsidised delivery options are available — please get in touch to discuss.
How quickly can a workshop be booked and delivered?
Subject to availability, workshops can typically be booked and delivered within 2–4 weeks. We begin with a discovery call and pre-workshop assessment to ensure the content is fully tailored to your team before delivery.

Still Have Questions?

Just ask. There is no wrong question and no obligation — just an honest conversation about what you need and how we can help.
