About Nuevo Ascend

The Story Behind The Work

Every consultancy has an origin. Ours begins at dawn, on a street corner, with an eleven-year-old boy delivering newspapers in the dark.

Leon Francis
Founder &
Director

"I was a manager at 16 with no mentor, no roadmap, and no one who looked like me at the top. I figured it out — and spent the next 20 years building the playbook I wish I'd had."

Nuevo Ascend was not built from a business plan. It was built from a life — a 20-year leadership career that took its founder from a paper boy at eleven, to a manager at sixteen, to hosting visits with Apple CEO Tim Cook, to winning the Black British Business Award in 2024.

Leon Francis has led at every level of the organisations he has worked in. Multi-site retail operations at Sainsbury's and Argos, including serving as Strategic Advisor to CEO Simon Roberts on inclusion matters. Senior Manager at Apple across Operations, CX and B2B relationship management, and head of one of the largest diversity networks across the UK.

In 2024, Leon won the Black British Business Award in the Consumer and Luxury category — an award sponsored by the Financial Times — for the success at Apple and of Nuevo Noir Ltd.
