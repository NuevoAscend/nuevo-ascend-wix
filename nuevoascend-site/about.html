About Nuevo Ascend

The Story Behind The Work

Every consultancy has an origin. Ours begins at dawn, on a street corner, with an eleven-year-old boy delivering newspapers in the dark.

Leon Francis
Founder &
Director

"I was a manager at 16 with no mentor, no roadmap, and no one who looked like me at the top. I figured it out — and spent the next 20 years building the playbook I wish I'd had."

Nuevo Ascend was not built from a business plan. It was built from a life — a 20-year leadership career that took its founder from a paper round at eleven, to a store management role at sixteen, to the stage at Apple with CEO Tim Cook, to winning the Black British Business Award in 2024.

Leon Francis has led at every level of the organisations he has worked in. Multi-site retail operations at Sainsbury's and Argos. Senior customer experience and B2B relationship management at Apple. Diversity and inclusion programme leadership across the UK and Ireland. Every role, every difficult conversation, every performance challenge, every team he has built and led — it is all in the work he does now.

The insight behind Nuevo Ascend is simple: the most valuable leadership development does not come from a framework someone read in a textbook. It comes from someone who has genuinely stood where you are standing — and found a way through. That is what Nuevo Ascend offers. Not theory. Lived experience, packaged into coaching, consultancy, and programmes that create real, lasting change.

In 2024, Leon's entrepreneurial journey was recognised with the Black British Business Award in the Consumer and Luxury category — an award sponsored by the Financial Times — for the success of Nuevo Noir Ltd, the sister business within the Nuevo Group. It was validation of a career built on refusing to accept the ceiling others set.

The Journey

From Paper Boy
To Tim Cook

AGE 11First Job

The Paper Round

At eleven years old, Leon Francis was up before dawn delivering newspapers. It was his first lesson in discipline, reliability, and showing up — every single day, regardless of how you feel. The work ethic that built a 20-year career began here.

AGE 16UK Retail

Manager at Sixteen

Most people at sixteen are navigating school. Leon became a store manager — one of the youngest in UK retail — leading a team of adults with no manual, no mentor, and no roadmap. Just instinct, determination, and an innate understanding that leadership is about earning trust, not demanding it.

🏪 One of the Youngest Store Managers in UK Retail
20+ YEARSSainsbury's & Argos

Two Decades of Operational Leadership

Over two decades, Leon built and led large-scale retail operations across some of the UK's most demanding and high-profile organisations. Multi-site management. Team development. Commercial accountability. Leading hundreds of people through change, growth, and challenge. Every lesson about what makes leadership work — and what destroys it — was learned in these years.

APPLESenior Leadership

The Apple Chapter

Senior leadership at one of the most iconic companies on the planet. Customer experience at the highest level. B2B relationship management. Leading diversity and inclusion programmes across the UK and Ireland — not as a box-ticking exercise, but as genuine, sustained work to ensure the next generation of leaders looked different from the last.

And in that chapter came a moment that defines the journey: standing on a stage and shaking hands with Apple CEO Tim Cook. Not as a spectator. As someone who had earned the right to be in that room.

🤝 Recognised at Global Leadership Level — Tim Cook
2022Nuevo Group

Building Nuevo Noir

While holding a full-time senior career, Leon founded Nuevo Noir Ltd — a premium grooming brand built from nothing. It required every skill his career had developed: operational rigour, commercial instinct, brand building, and the ability to lead under pressure. Proof that entrepreneurship and operational excellence are the same discipline.

2024Black British Business Awards

BBBA Winner — Sponsored by the Financial Times

In 2024, Nuevo Noir won the Black British Business Award in the Consumer and Luxury category — an award sponsored by the Financial Times. It was not just recognition for a product. It was recognition for a career built on resilience, excellence, and refusing to accept the ceiling others set. The validation of everything the journey had built.

🏆 BBBA 2024 — Consumer & Luxury — Sponsored by the FT
2026Nuevo Ascend Ltd

The Next Chapter Begins

Nuevo Ascend Ltd was founded to package 20+ years of lived leadership into coaching, consultancy, workshops, and programmes that help others do what the journey proved was possible. The entire story — from paper round to Tim Cook — is now the product. And it is available to every individual and organisation ready to invest in what genuine leadership development looks like.
